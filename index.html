Cell segmentation and analysis, in the browser, on your GPU

Three microscopy segmentation models ported to hand-written WGSL — no PyTorch, no TensorFlow, no CUDA, no ML framework at inference time — plus a Python notebook that runs the real scientific stack locally under Pyodide. Serve this folder over http://localhost and open a page. WebGPU required: Chrome or Edge, or Safari 18+.

📓 Notebook

numpy · pandas · scikit-image · matplotlib running in the tab, with all three segmentation models callable from Python under their real library names. Point it at a folder on your disk and your files never leave the machine.

Cellpose cyto3

Generalist cytoplasm segmentation. CNN forward pass plus flow dynamics, with cellpose-exact tiled inference.

StarDist 2D_versatile_fluo · 2D_versatile_he

Star-convex nuclei segmentation: U-Net → radial distances → polygon NMS. The fluo checkpoint is for fluorescence nuclei, the he checkpoint for H&E histology.

InstanSeg brightfield_nuclei

Embedding-based instance segmentation: U-Net → learned pixel embeddings → instance decode. For brightfield and unstained nuclei.

Architecture notes and the hard-won gotchas live in docs/ARCHITECTURE.md and the README. Weights load lazily from weights/<model>/ — a session only downloads the models it actually uses. Point Model.load(url) at a CDN to serve them elsewhere.
